## Why finding 1 is accepted, not solved
Codex's conclusion: with today's primitives, an AMM that trades synchronously on the EVM cannot
safely run beside an order that settles asynchronously on Core, unless something tells the EVM
the order has run. Nothing does (docs: reads are snapshots taken when the EVM block is built;
CoreWriter orders are delayed for seconds). Rounds 1–6 all come back to this.

Decision (2026-10-17): pause for 30 s after each order, about 10× the fill delay we
measured, measure the delay on testnet, record the residual risk, and stop chasing APPROVE here.
If an order ever ran after 30 s, the most that can be lost is what a sandwich makes on that one
order (bounded by its size and the 1% limit).
